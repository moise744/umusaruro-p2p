## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Umusaruro P2P** (meaning "Harvest" in Kinyarwanda) is a next-generation, peer-to-peer agricultural investment and farm management platform built entirely in Flutter. It digitally connects smallholder farmers in rural communities with a growing class of retail micro-investors who want to participate directly in agricultural value chains.

The platform eliminates the friction of traditional agricultural financing by replacing bank collateral requirements with community-based verification, replacing opaque loan agreements with transparent investment contracts, and replacing manual harvest reporting with digitally logged, verifiable yield data.

**What makes Umusaruro P2P stand out:**
- Community-led verification by local Cell Leaders (unique trust layer)
- Real-time P2P investment with live funding progress tracking
- End-to-end harvest lifecycle management inside a single app
- Built-in encrypted messaging between farmers and investors
- Designed offline-first for rural connectivity realities
- AI-powered harvest yield prediction and fraud detection
- Multi-currency support including Mobile Money (MoMo), MTN, Airtel

## 2. VISION & STRATEGIC GOALS

### 2.1 Mission Statement
To democratize agricultural finance by building a trusted, transparent, and technology-powered bridge between farmers who need capital and investors who want impact-driven returns.

### 2.2 Strategic Goals

**Goal 1 — Financial Inclusion:** Enable farmers with zero banking history to access project funding within 72 hours of cell leader verification.

**Goal 2 — Investor Confidence:** Give every investor a real-time, auditable view of their portfolio including fund usage, farm activity updates, and expected ROI timelines.

**Goal 3 — Community Trust:** Build a decentralized reputation system where cell leaders, farmers, and investors all earn trust scores that improve their platform standing over time.

**Goal 4 — Scalability:** Design the system to scale from a single district to a national and eventually pan-African deployment, supporting multiple languages (Kinyarwanda, French, English, Swahili).

**Goal 5 — Data Sovereignty:** All farmer data is owned by the farmer. Investors see only what is explicitly shared. No third-party data selling.

## 3. USER PERSONAS & ROLES

### 3.1 Role: FARMER

**Who they are:** Smallholder or mid-scale farmer, typically aged 22–55, with 1–10 hectares of farmland. May be semi-literate; relies heavily on visual UI and voice-assisted features.

**Their core needs:**
- Fast, low-friction project creation (under 10 minutes)
- Clear visibility of how much funding has been received
- Simple harvest reporting (photo upload + weight input)
- Direct communication with their investors
- Offline access to all their active project data

**Permissions & Capabilities:**
- Create, edit, pause, or archive farming projects
- Upload land photos, certificates, and soil reports
- Submit weekly farming activity updates (text + photo)
- Log harvest yield and trigger payout calculation
- Message any investor who has funded their project
- View full transaction history (received funds, payouts sent)
- Access personal reputation score and badge history

**Account States:** `Unverified → Identity Verified → Cell Leader Approved → Active`

---

### 3.2 Role: INVESTOR

**Who they are:** Urban professional, diaspora investor, or impact-focused individual aged 20–60. Comfortable with mobile apps. May invest in multiple projects simultaneously.

**Their core needs:**
- A curated, trustworthy marketplace of verified projects
- Portfolio dashboard showing live ROI tracking
- Transparent communication with farmers
- Secure, traceable investment and return transactions
- Notifications at every key milestone

**Permissions & Capabilities:**
- Browse and filter all verified, live investment projects
- Invest any amount within a project's minimum/maximum range
- View real-time funding progress per project
- Receive push notifications on farm activity updates
- Access full investment ledger with expected return dates
- Withdraw returns to registered mobile money or bank account
- Rate and review farmers after project completion
- Bookmark projects to a personal watchlist

**Account States:** `Registered → KYC Verified → Active Investor`

---

### 3.3 Role: CELL LEADER (VERIFIER)

**Who they are:** Elected or appointed local administrative official. Acts as the trust anchor for the system. Knows farmers personally and can verify land ownership physically.

**Their core needs:**
- Simple, guided verification workflow
- Ability to raise flags or report suspicious projects
- A log of all verifications they have performed

**Permissions & Capabilities:**
- Receive verification requests from farmers in their cell
- Review farmer profile, submitted land documents, and GPS coordinates
- Approve, reject, or request more information on a project
- Attach a digital signature and official notes to verification
- Revoke verification if fraud is later discovered
- View history of all verified projects and their current status
- Cannot invest or create projects (conflict of interest prevention)

**Account States:** `Nominated → Identity Verified by Admin → Active Verifier`

---

### 3.4 Role: PLATFORM ADMIN (Back-end / CMS)

**Who they are:** Umusaruro operations team member managing the system health, compliance, and disputes.

**Capabilities:**
- Onboard, suspend, or ban any user account
- Resolve disputes between farmers and investors
- View full platform analytics (active projects, funds in circulation, defaults)
- Manually trigger or block payouts
- Manage Cell Leader appointments
- Access audit logs for all financial transactions

## 4. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW

### 4.1 Technology Stack

| Layer | Technology |
|---|---|
| Mobile App | Flutter 3.x (Dart) |
| State Management | Riverpod + AsyncNotifier |
| Local Storage | Hive + Isar (for structured offline data) |
| Backend | superbase (Auth, Firestore, Storage, Functions) OR Supabase (preferred for SQL) |
| Real-Time Chat | superbase Realtime Database OR Stream Chat SDK |
| Push Notifications | superbase Cloud Messaging (FCM) |
| Payment Gateway | MTN Mobile Money API, Airtel Money API, Flutterwave |
| Maps & GPS | Google Maps Flutter Plugin |
| AI / ML | Google ML Kit (on-device) + OpenAI API (server-side predictions) |
| Analytics | superbase Analytics + Mixpanel |
| Crash Reporting | Sentry or superbase Crashlytics |
| CI/CD | GitHub Actions + Fastlane |

### 4.2 Architecture Pattern
The app follows **Clean Architecture** with three distinct layers:

- **Presentation Layer:** Flutter Widgets, Screens, ViewModels (Riverpod Providers)
- **Domain Layer:** Use Cases, Entities, Repository Interfaces
- **Data Layer:** Remote Data Sources (API/superbase), Local Data Sources (Hive/Isar), Repository Implementations

### 4.3 Core Principles
- **Offline-First:** All reads served from local cache. Writes queued and synced when online.
- **Reactive UI:** All state updates propagate instantly using streams and providers.
- **Modular Codebase:** Each feature (auth, projects, chat, wallet) is an isolated module.
- **Security by Default:** No sensitive data stored in plain text. All API keys in secure environment files.

## 5. FEATURE REQUIREMENTS (FUNCTIONAL)

### MODULE 1: AUTHENTICATION & ONBOARDING

**F-AUTH-01:** The app shall present a splash screen with the Umusaruro logo and animated tagline for 2–3 seconds before routing based on auth state.

**F-AUTH-02:** First-time users shall see an interactive onboarding carousel (3–5 slides) explaining the platform in Kinyarwanda and English with illustrations.

**F-AUTH-03:** Users shall register with a phone number (OTP-verified via SMS), email address (optional), full name, national ID number, and role selection (Farmer / Investor).

**F-AUTH-04:** Phone OTP verification shall be completed within 60 seconds with a resend option after 30 seconds.

**F-AUTH-05:** After OTP, users shall set a 6-digit PIN for in-app transaction authorization (distinct from login password).

**F-AUTH-06:** Biometric login (fingerprint / Face ID) shall be available after first successful PIN login.

**F-AUTH-07:** Farmers shall complete an extended profile including: district/sector/cell location, farming experience (years), land size (hectares), primary crops grown, and a profile photo.

**F-AUTH-08:** Investors shall complete KYC by uploading a national ID or passport photo (front and back). KYC review status shall be shown as a progress indicator.

**F-AUTH-09:** Cell Leaders are appointed by admins only. They receive a system-generated invite link to register under the Cell Leader role.

**F-AUTH-10:** The system shall enforce account lockout after 5 consecutive failed PIN attempts, requiring OTP re-verification to unlock.

---

### MODULE 2: FARMER — PROJECT MANAGEMENT

**F-PROJ-01:** A Farmer shall create a new farming project through a guided, multi-step form wizard (5 steps, progress bar shown).

**Step 1 — Project Basics:**
- Project name (e.g., "Season A Maize Farm — Musanze")
- Crop type (dropdown with icons: Maize, Potato, Beans, Rice, Wheat, Other)
- Season (A, B, C) and planting start date
- Expected harvest date (calendar picker)

**Step 2 — Location:**
- District, Sector, Cell (cascading dropdowns pre-loaded from Rwanda administrative data)
- GPS pin drop on embedded Google Map
- Land size in hectares (numeric slider + input)
- Option to upload land ownership certificate (PDF/image)

**Step 3 — Financial Details:**
- Total funding needed (RWF)
- Minimum investment per investor (RWF)
- Maximum investment per investor (RWF, optional)
- Expected total return percentage (e.g., 20% above capital)
- Profit sharing model: Pro-rata based on investment share

**Step 4 — Evidence Upload:**
- Up to 6 land photos (enforced minimum: 2)
- Optional: previous season's harvest receipt (for experienced farmers)
- Optional: soil test report

**Step 5 — Review & Submit:**
- Full summary of all entered data
- Terms & Conditions checkbox
- Submit for Cell Leader verification

**F-PROJ-02:** After submission, the project status becomes `PENDING_VERIFICATION`. The farmer receives a push notification and in-app confirmation.

**F-PROJ-03:** Farmers shall be able to edit projects only while in `PENDING_VERIFICATION` or `DRAFT` status.

**F-PROJ-04:** Farmers shall view all their projects on a "My Projects" screen with filterable status tabs: All / Draft / Pending / Active / Funded / Harvested / Completed.

**F-PROJ-05:** Each project card shall display: project name, crop icon, funding progress bar (% and RWF), days remaining to funding deadline, and current status badge.

**F-PROJ-06:** Farmers shall post "Farm Updates" (text + up to 3 photos) on any active project. Updates are visible to all investors of that project.

**F-PROJ-07:** Farmers shall submit harvest data including: actual harvest yield in kilograms, sale price per kilogram, total revenue generated, and up to 5 harvest photos. The system auto-calculates investor returns.

**F-PROJ-08:** If a project fails to reach 100% funding by its deadline, the system shall automatically refund all invested amounts to investors with zero deductions.

---

### MODULE 3: CELL LEADER — VERIFICATION WORKFLOW

**F-VERIF-01:** Cell Leaders receive a push notification immediately when a farmer in their assigned cell submits a project for verification.

**F-VERIF-02:** The verification screen shall display all project details submitted by the farmer including embedded map with GPS coordinates and all uploaded photos in a scrollable gallery.

**F-VERIF-03:** The Cell Leader shall have three actions: **Approve**, **Request More Info**, or **Reject**.

**F-VERIF-04:** All three actions require a mandatory written note (minimum 20 characters) explaining the decision.

**F-VERIF-05:** Upon approval, the project status changes to `ACTIVE` and it immediately appears in the Investor marketplace. The farmer is notified instantly.

**F-VERIF-06:** Upon rejection, the farmer is notified with the Cell Leader's reason. The farmer may correct and resubmit. Maximum 2 resubmissions per project.

**F-VERIF-07:** The Cell Leader's verification dashboard shall show: pending requests (sorted by submission date), completed verifications this month, and their personal verification track record (approval rate, flagged projects).

**F-VERIF-08:** Cell Leaders shall be able to mark a previously verified project as "Under Investigation" if fraud is suspected post-approval, which temporarily pauses new investments.

---

### MODULE 4: INVESTOR — MARKETPLACE & INVESTMENTS

**F-INV-01:** The marketplace screen shall display all `ACTIVE` projects in a card-based feed with infinite scroll.

**F-INV-02:** Each project card shall show: crop photo, farmer name, location, funding progress bar, % funded, RWF needed, expected ROI %, and days left to invest.

**F-INV-03:** Investors shall filter projects by: crop type, location (district), funding stage (early / mid / nearly full), expected return range, and time to harvest.

**F-INV-04:** Investors shall sort projects by: newest, highest return, closest to funding goal, shortest harvest timeline.

**F-INV-05:** Tapping a project opens a full Project Detail Screen showing all farmer-submitted information, Cell Leader verification badge and notes, all farm photos in a swipeable gallery, a funding breakdown chart, investor list (anonymous or named by preference), and all farm update posts.

**F-INV-06:** The investment flow shall be: Enter Amount → Review Summary → Enter 6-digit PIN → Confirm → Payment Processing → Success Screen with confetti animation.

**F-INV-07:** Investors shall be able to invest in a project multiple times as long as the project is still in `ACTIVE` status and they have not exceeded the project's maximum investment cap.

**F-INV-08:** The investor's portfolio dashboard shall display: total amount invested (all time), total active investments, total returns received, ROI percentage across all completed projects, and a pie chart of investment distribution by crop type.

**F-INV-09:** Investors shall have a "Watchlist" feature to save projects they want to track before investing.

**F-INV-10:** When a farm update is posted by a farmer, all investors who have funded that project receive a push notification with a preview.

---

### MODULE 5: REAL-TIME MESSAGING

**F-CHAT-01:** Once an investor funds a project, a dedicated chat thread is automatically created between that investor and the farmer.

**F-CHAT-02:** The chat screen shall support: text messages, image attachments (camera or gallery), and voice notes (up to 60 seconds).

**F-CHAT-03:** Messages shall display sent, delivered, and read receipts.

**F-CHAT-04:** The chat shall display a "Project Context Bar" at the top showing the project name, crop, and funding status so both parties always know which project the conversation is about.

**F-CHAT-05:** Farmers who have multiple investors shall see a "Messages" screen showing all their active conversation threads, sorted by most recent message.

**F-CHAT-06:** All messages shall be end-to-end encrypted. Message content shall never be stored in plain text on the server.

**F-CHAT-07:** Users shall be able to report abusive messages. Reported chats are flagged for Admin review. Repeat offenders face automatic account suspension.

**F-CHAT-08:** The platform shall display a "Smart Reply" suggestion bar with contextual quick responses (e.g., "When do you expect to harvest?" / "The crops are doing well, thank you!").

---

### MODULE 6: WALLET & TRANSACTIONS

**F-WALLET-01:** Every user (Farmer and Investor) has a platform wallet displaying their available balance.

**F-WALLET-02:** Investors can top up their wallet via: MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money, Bank Transfer (via Flutterwave), or debit card.

**F-WALLET-03:** When an investor invests, funds move from their wallet to a project escrow account. The project escrow is released to the farmer upon reaching 100% funding.

**F-WALLET-04:** The transaction history screen shall display: date, time, transaction type (Top-up / Investment / Return / Withdrawal / Refund), amount, status (Completed / Pending / Failed), and a reference ID.

**F-WALLET-05:** When a farmer submits harvest data and the platform admin approves the payout, the investor's proportional return is auto-credited to their wallet.

**F-WALLET-06:** Withdrawals to mobile money shall process within 24 hours. Users receive an SMS and push notification upon completion.

**F-WALLET-07:** All financial actions (invest, withdraw) require 6-digit PIN authorization.

**F-WALLET-08:** The platform shall charge a transaction fee of X% (configurable by admin) on investor returns only — never on principal. Farmers pay a platform commission of Y% on harvest revenue (configurable).

---

### MODULE 7: NOTIFICATIONS

**F-NOTIF-01:** Push notifications shall be sent for: new project verification request (Cell Leader), project approved / rejected (Farmer), project fully funded (Farmer), new investment received (Farmer), farm update posted (Investor), harvest submitted (Investor), return credited (Investor), new message received (All), system announcements (All).

**F-NOTIF-02:** In-app notification bell icon shall show unread count badge.

**F-NOTIF-03:** The notifications screen shall show all past notifications with timestamp, categorized as: Financial, Project, Messages, System.

**F-NOTIF-04:** Users shall manage notification preferences per category in Settings.

## 6. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

### 6.1 Performance

**NFR-PERF-01:** App cold start time shall be under 3 seconds on a mid-range Android device (2GB RAM, Android 10+).

**NFR-PERF-02:** The marketplace feed shall load within 1.5 seconds on a 3G connection.

**NFR-PERF-03:** Chat messages shall be delivered and displayed within 500ms on a 4G connection.

**NFR-PERF-04:** All screens shall maintain a minimum 60fps frame rate. Jank frames (>16ms) shall not exceed 1% of total frames.

**NFR-PERF-05:** Image assets shall be compressed and lazy-loaded. No image exceeding 500KB shall be stored or displayed without compression.

### 6.2 Reliability

**NFR-REL-01:** The platform backend shall maintain 99.5% uptime per month (excluding planned maintenance).

**NFR-REL-02:** All financial transactions shall be atomic. Partial transaction states are not allowed. Failed transactions must be fully rolled back.

**NFR-REL-03:** The app shall handle network drops gracefully without crashing. Offline state triggers a non-blocking banner.

### 6.3 Scalability

**NFR-SCALE-01:** The system shall support up to 100,000 concurrent users without degradation in API response times.

**NFR-SCALE-02:** The database shall be architected to support horizontal scaling (sharding by district/region for future national rollout).

### 6.4 Usability

**NFR-USE-01:** The app shall achieve a System Usability Scale (SUS) score of at least 75 in user testing.

**NFR-USE-02:** All primary user tasks (create project, invest, send message) shall be completable in under 5 taps from the dashboard.

**NFR-USE-03:** The app shall support Kinyarwanda as the default language, with English and French as selectable alternatives. All UI strings shall be fully localized using Flutter's `intl` package.

**NFR-USE-04:** The app shall comply with WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards: minimum contrast ratio 4.5:1, all interactive elements tappable with minimum 48×48dp touch targets, screen reader (TalkBack/VoiceOver) compatible.

### 6.5 Security

**NFR-SEC-01:** All API communications shall use HTTPS/TLS 1.3.

**NFR-SEC-02:** Sensitive data (PIN, national ID number) shall never be stored in device SharedPreferences or plain local storage. Use Flutter Secure Storage (Keychain / Keystore).

**NFR-SEC-03:** The app shall implement certificate pinning to prevent man-in-the-middle attacks.

**NFR-SEC-04:** All financial operations shall require re-authentication (PIN or biometric) regardless of active session status.

**NFR-SEC-05:** Session tokens shall expire after 7 days of inactivity. Refresh tokens expire after 30 days.

## 9. ADVANCED FEATURE SPECIFICATIONS

### 9.1 Reputation & Trust Score System

Every user accumulates a **Trust Score (0–100)** that is displayed on their profile and project listings.

**Farmer Trust Score is influenced by:**
- Number of completed projects (+ points)
- On-time harvest submissions (+ points)
- Investor ratings received after project completion (+ points)
- Late or missing updates (- points)
- Projects that defaulted without reason (- severe penalty)
- Cell Leader verification badge (+ bonus)

**Investor Trust Score is influenced by:**
- Total invested (all time)
- Investment completion rate (vs. abandoned carts)
- Respectful communication score (from farmer feedback)

**Trust Score Tiers:** Bronze (0–40) / Silver (41–65) / Gold (66–85) / Platinum (86–100)

Platinum farmers get featured placement in the marketplace. Platinum investors get early access to new projects.

---

### 9.2 AI-Powered Yield Prediction

Using historical crop data + external weather API integration:

- When a farmer enters their crop type, land size, and location, the system runs a **yield prediction model** and shows an estimated harvest range (e.g., "Based on past seasons and current weather in Musanze, your maize farm is estimated to yield 2.2–2.8 tonnes").
- This prediction is shown to investors on the project detail screen as an AI confidence indicator.
- The model is updated after each completed harvest to improve accuracy.

**Tech:** On-device lightweight model via Google ML Kit for basic predictions; server-side inference via superbase Functions + OpenAI API for advanced analysis.

---

### 9.3 Smart Fraud Detection

The platform shall run automated checks at three critical points:

**At project submission:**
- GPS coordinates cross-referenced with satellite imagery API (check land type is actually agricultural)
- Duplicate land detection (same GPS coordinates used in multiple active projects by different farmers)
- Farming pattern analysis (unusually high return promises flagged for manual review)

**During funding phase:**
- Investor account velocity checks (abnormal investment patterns)
- Self-investment prevention (farmer cannot invest in their own project)

**At harvest submission:**
- Yield reasonableness check (flagged if yield is 3x higher than prediction)
- Harvest revenue plausibility check (flagged if sale price is far outside market rates)

All flagged items are surfaced to admin for review before payouts are processed.

---

- **Kinyarwanda (default):** Full UI translation, including all form labels, error messages, notifications, and onboarding content.
- **English:** Full UI translation.

- All number formatting adapts to locale (RWF currency format, date formats).

## 10. SECURITY & COMPLIANCE

### 10.1 Data Privacy
- The platform shall comply with Rwanda's **Law No. 058/2021 on the Protection of Personal Data and Privacy**.
- All users must explicitly consent to data collection and usage at registration.
- Users can request data export (all their personal data in JSON format) and account deletion at any time from Settings.

### 10.2 Financial Compliance
- The platform operates under the oversight of the **National Bank of Rwanda (BNR)** regulations for digital payment service providers.
- All transactions are recorded with full audit trails accessible to regulators upon request.
- KYC verification is mandatory for any user transacting above a defined threshold.

### 10.3 Security Measures Summary
- HTTPS/TLS 1.3 for all data in transit

## 12. OFFLINE-FIRST STRATEGY

The app is architected so that farmers in rural areas with poor or no connectivity can continue using the app meaningfully.

**What works fully offline:**
- Viewing all previously loaded projects (farmer and investor)
- Reading all chat messages that have been loaded
- Composing draft farm updates (saved locally, synced when online)
- Viewing wallet balance and transaction history (last synced state)
- Accessing all project documents and photos (cached locally)

**What requires connectivity:**
- Submitting new projects or updates (queued and auto-sent when online)
- Making investments or withdrawals
- Sending or receiving new chat messages
- Loading new marketplace projects not yet in cache

**Connectivity Indicators:**
- Persistent non-blocking banner: "You are offline. Some features are limited."
- Banner turns green: "Back online. Syncing your data…"
- Queued actions show a pending indicator with count (e.g., "2 updates waiting to sync")

**Sync Strategy:**
- Uses Isar or Hive for local data persistence
- Background sync via WorkManager (Android) and BGTaskScheduler (iOS)
- Conflict resolution: server state always wins for financial data; local state wins for draft content

## 14. MONETIZATION & BUSINESS LOGIC

### 14.1 Revenue Streams

**Platform Commission (Primary):** A configurable % of investor returns (e.g., 5%) charged at payout. This is deducted automatically before crediting investors.

**Farmer Subscription (Secondary):** Farmers with more than 3 active projects simultaneously pay a small monthly plan fee for a "Pro Farmer" tier with higher photo upload limits and featured placement.

**Premium Investor Tier:** Investors unlock advanced analytics, early project access, and detailed farm reports for a monthly fee.

**B2B Data Insights (Future):** Aggregated, anonymized crop yield and pricing data sold to agricultural organizations, insurers, and government bodies.

### 14.2 Return Calculation Logic
```
Investor Share % = Investor Amount / Total Project Funding Goal × 100
Expected Return = Investor Amount × (1 + Project Expected Return %)
Actual Return = Harvest Revenue × Investor Share % − Platform Fee
Net Return to Investor = Actual Return (credited to wallet)
```

### 14.3 Escrow Logic
- Investor funds are held in a per-project escrow account.
- Funds are released to the farmer **only** when funding reaches 100%.
- If a project is cancelled or fails to fund by deadline, all escrow funds are returned in full automatically within 24 hours.
- Harvest payouts require Admin approval before release to prevent fraud.
